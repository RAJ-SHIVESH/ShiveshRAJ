01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

From historicals to a forward view.

This independent project applies a structured FP&A and corporate finance approach to Danone’s publicly reported financial information. It is an analytical exercise and is not commissioned, reviewed or endorsed by Danone.

01

Historical analysis

Reconstructed the 2021A–2025A financial statements and reviewed performance, margins, working capital and key financial ratios.

02

Integrated model

Linked the income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement with supporting debt, cash, interest, PP&E and working capital schedules.

03

Forecasting

Developed a five-year 2026E–2030E forecast using historical ratios, operating assumptions and selectable sales-growth scenarios.
